# Security reporting

Do not disclose a suspected vulnerability, exposed identity record, payment discrepancy or fraudulent credential in a public issue.

For the official pilot, report the incident to the UCC contact configured as `SUPPORT_EMAIL`. Include the affected page, time, account email and a concise description. Do not email passwords, payment card details or full identity numbers.

The operations lead should preserve relevant audit records, restrict affected access, rotate exposed secrets, notify the approved UCC incident team and document the decision and recovery actions.
